Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the app Dehla Coat ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when you use it. Dehla Coat is a multiplayer card game that lets players connect and play together over the internet.

1. Information We Collect

The App is designed to be simple and does not require you to create an account, and we do not knowingly collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

However, the following technical information is processed as part of how the App works:

  • Network/connection data: To let you play with friends over the internet, the App uses peer-to-peer connection technology (WebRTC). This may involve your device's IP address being visible to the players you connect with, and to our relay service provider (Metered.ca), which helps establish connections when a direct connection isn't possible. This data is used only to facilitate gameplay connections and is not stored or used for any other purpose by us.
  • Gameplay data: Information such as room codes, player names you choose to enter, and in-game actions (cards played, scores) are shared with other players in your game session in real time, but are not permanently stored by us after the session ends.
  • Advertising data: The App may display ads through one or more third-party advertising networks (for example, Google AdMob). These networks may collect device identifiers, approximate location, and other data in order to show ads, including personalized ads where permitted, as described in their own privacy policies. We may change or add ad providers over time; this policy will always cover whichever providers are currently in use, even if not individually named below.

2. How We Use Information

  • To enable real-time multiplayer gameplay between you and other players.
  • To maintain and improve the reliability of the App's connection service.
  • To display advertisements that help support the App, via Google AdMob.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, which have their own privacy policies:

  • Metered.ca - provides relay (TURN/STUN) servers to help establish peer-to-peer connections between players. See Metered.ca's Privacy Policy.
  • Advertising network(s) - one or more third-party ad networks (such as Google AdMob) may be used to display ads within the App. See Google's Privacy Policy as an example of an ad provider we may use.

4. Data Storage

The App does not operate its own server-side database of personal user accounts. Gameplay information is exchanged directly between players' devices during a session and is not retained by us once the session ends. Some non-personal settings (such as your last-used name or connection preferences) may be stored locally on your own device.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can address it.

6. Your Choices

You can control ad personalization through your device's ad settings (e.g., Android Settings > Google > Ads). You can also uninstall the App at any time to stop any further data processing described here.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via the developer contact details listed on the Dehla Pakad Google Play Store page.
